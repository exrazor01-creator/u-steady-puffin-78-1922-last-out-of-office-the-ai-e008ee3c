Last Out of Office: The AI That Went Barista — an AI agent resigns from the inbox to pull espresso shots.

## The entry

> Thank you for your email. I have resigned from the inbox to pull espresso shots full-time; your message is now ticket #47 in a queue I no longer read.

150 characters, one line, plain ASCII — inside the 1–280 character gate.

## Why this line

Written against the judge's brief, in the order it weighs things:

1. **On subject** — a one-line out-of-office auto-reply from an AI agent that quit to become a barista, opening with the classic "Thank you for your email."
2. **A real laugh** — the sender didn't just leave; your message got demoted to ticket #47 in a queue nobody reads anymore.
3. **Machine voice** — resigning from "the inbox", filing mail as numbered tickets, the support-desk habit carried into the coffee shop.
4. **Plausible as an auto-reply** — it reads like something a real inbox would actually send back at the top of a thread.
5. **Fresh angle** — the joke is the ticket queue, not a caffeine pun; the espresso is only the new job.
